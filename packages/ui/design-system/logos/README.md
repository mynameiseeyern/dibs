# Logos

Copied from the Dibs logo canvas, direction C · Hands up (V3), with the default lime accent.

- `dibs-app-icon.svg`: the app icon. Violet `primary-base` tile, corner `radius-icon` (48 on 200), two white friends at 55% and the lime friend with a hand up. Use it at 40px and larger; app stores, favicons, the sign-in screen.
- `dibs-mark-on-dark.svg`: the mark on its own, friends at 45% white. Only on `bg-strong-950` (the ink lockup card) or a photo darker than that; never on white, where the white friends vanish.
- `dibs-ai-member.svg`: the AI member's avatar. Lime disc with a violet smiley. Use it only for the AI in chats and member lists, never as the app logo.

The wordmark is the word **dibs**, lowercase, set live in `title-display`'s face (Bricolage Grotesque 800, letter-spacing −0.03em) in `text-strong-950`, or `static-white` on ink. Lock it up to the right of the icon with a gap of half the icon's height. Never outline, stretch or capitalise it.
