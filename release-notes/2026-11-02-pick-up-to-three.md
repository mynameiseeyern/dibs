# What's new in Dibby

Thanks for being on the beta. A lot's landed since you signed up, so here's the good stuff. Reply to this email if anything's off, it comes straight to us.

## Pick up to three

Can't choose between ramen and dumplings? You don't have to. On a vote you can now pick up to three places, and most votes still wins. You always get one fewer pick than there are places, so nobody ticks the lot and the vote actually means something. Organisers can switch it back to one pick each in Edit plan.

## Your plans, in your calendar

Subscribe once from [your account](https://dibby.social/account#calendar) and every plan you're in shows up in your calendar when it locks in. Time moves? It updates. Called off, or you can't make it? It drops off. Works with Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook.

## Making a plan is quicker

One question a screen, and you don't need an account to start: just your name, then share the link. Not sure when? Ask the crew which day works and they tap the days they're free.

## Spots, plus-ones and a waitlist

Plans are open by default. Want to cap it at 10? Set the spots, and anyone after that joins a waitlist that moves up on its own when someone drops out. People who are in can bring a plus-one too, with a name if they like.

## Only what you need

Not every hang needs a group chat or a bill split. Organisers can switch off the chat, photos, bills or the recap on any plan, and switch them back on later.

## Dibby camera

Everyone can snap up to three photos during a plan. They stay hidden until wrap-up, then you all see them at once.

## Smaller things

- The bill game is now **spin the wheel**, and the whole crew watches it go round live
- The plan page is one clean page, with the next step pinned at the bottom
- Wrap up a plan whenever it's done, and add someone who rocked up last minute
- Lost your spot on a new browser? Your rejoin link gets you back, no email needed
- The weather shows on your plan if it has a time and a place, up to 10 days out
- We only ask for your location when Dibby's actually searching nearby
- Light or dark, your call, in your account
