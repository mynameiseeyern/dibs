# The app's here (in beta)

You put your name down for the app, so you're first in. It's free, like everything else.

## Get it in three taps

1. Open [dibby.social/app](https://dibby.social/app) on your iPhone.
2. Tap **Get TestFlight** (Apple's app for trying betas), then **Join the Dibby beta**.
3. Open it and sign in with this email. Your plans are already there.

On a computer? That page has a QR code. Point your phone's camera at it.

Android's coming soon. We'll email you when it's ready.

## New in the app: crews

For the group you're always organising. Make a crew once and every plan lives in one spot, in date order, with what's still being decided up top.

- **Invite with a link.** They tap it, ask to join, you say yes.
- **Plans just for the crew.** Or open one up to anyone with the link.
- **Same people again?** After a couple of plans with the same lot, you can turn them into a crew in one tap.

## It's a beta

Things will break. When they do, take a screenshot in the app and TestFlight asks if you want to send it to us. Or just reply to this email, it comes straight to us.
