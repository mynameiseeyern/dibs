# What's new in dibs

Thanks for trying the beta. Here's what landed this week. Reply to this email if anything's off, it comes straight to us.

## Add it to your calendar

Once a plan's locked in, tap **Add to calendar** and pick Apple, Google or Outlook. Works on any phone or laptop.

## The weather, right on the plan

If your plan has a time and a place, dibs shows the forecast for it, up to 10 days out. Handy for that rooftop booking.

## Roulette everyone can watch

When someone spins for the bill, the whole crew sees the wheel go round live. Loser shouts, no take-backs.

## Smaller things

- RSVPs answer your tap straight away
- Ask **@dibby** in a thread to start the next plan: same crew, three venues to vote on
- Your plans page is tidier when nobody owes anything

> Got an idea or found a bug? Just hit reply.
